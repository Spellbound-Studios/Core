# The Unity ledger

The studio's judgement of every Unity system against what the line builds: Moved means Unity's replaces ours and ours is deleted, Builds on means ours sits on Unity's mechanism, Keeps means Unity has nothing for the job, Watch names the trigger that makes us re-judge. Core's own rows are in its AGENTS.md; a package's rows live in its AGENTS.md; the check is core unity-ledger. A code owner reviews every change to a judgement.
